The Bureau of Indian Standards

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is India's National Standards Body, established under the BIS Act, 2016. It is responsible for the harmonious development of standardisation, marking and quality certification of goods, and for matters connected with it. BIS operates from its headquarters in New Delhi with a network of regional and branch offices, and laboratories across the country.

Standards formulation

Technical committees of subject-matter experts draft and periodically revise Indian Standards across every major industry sector.

Product certification

The ISI mark scheme licenses manufacturers whose products and processes conform to the relevant Indian Standard.

Hallmarking

BIS certifies the purity of gold and silver jewellery sold in India through a network of registered Assaying & Hallmarking Centres.

Laboratory services

Regional and recognised third-party laboratories carry out conformity testing that underpins every certification scheme.

Training & outreach

The BIS Training Institute runs programmes on standardisation and quality management for students and industry.

Consumer protection

A public grievance channel lets consumers report substandard or counterfeit ISI/Hallmark-labelled products.
