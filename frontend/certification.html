The ISI mark, explained

The ISI mark is India's most recognised product-quality certification, confirming a product conforms to the applicable Indian Standard through independent testing and factory audits.

About the Service

BIS certification ensures that products meet Indian Standards for quality and reliability. It helps build trust and opens access to wider markets.
